1 · The problem

Discounts exist. Nobody tells you where.

Banks and wallets sign deals with shops and restaurants, but the offer sits in a leaflet, an SMS or an app you never open. You pay full price, then find out later.

Discounts walked past: 0

2 · The solution

Your phone speaks up at the right moment.

Every participating place has an invisible circle around it. Step inside and you get a notification with the exact offer on your card or wallet. Proximity is worked out on your phone.

3 · The iOS app

Scroll through the app.

  1. Sign in and get startedCreate your account in the app or sign in, then unlock with Face ID next time.
  2. Discover offers on the mapFilter by All, Mine, General or a bank. Category pins, a New offers carousel, Near you chips, and tabs for Home, Favourites, Alerts and Profile.
  3. Add your cards and walletsBCI, Absa, M-Pesa, e-Mola, mKesh and more. Only the product, never the number, expiry or CVV.
  4. Choose your alertsOnly my cards, mine plus chosen banks, or all offers, with quiet hours when you want peace.

Available to test on iOS through TestFlight.

4 · Made for your wallet

Add your cards. We never ask for the numbers.

Pick your bank cards and wallets such as BCI, Absa, Millennium bim, M-Pesa, e-Mola and mKesh. We never collect a card number, expiry date or CVV. Only the product you hold.

You only hear about discounts your own cards and wallets unlock.

5 · The web app

Everything, managed in one place.

  1. One front doorSign in, create an account or open the public discount map with no account. Portuguese first, English one tap away.
  2. Business profile and approvalBanks, wallet providers and merchants set up their profile. An admin approves it before offers go live.
  3. Premises and alert radiusAdd places on a map or import a CSV. Each place gets its own alert radius, such as 150 m.
  4. Discounts from draft to expiredA step-by-step wizard, per-card and per-wallet benefits, schedules, preview and bulk actions. Draft, published, paused, expired, archived.
  5. Reports and admin toolsStatistics, Excel exports, business approvals, the card and wallet catalogue, users and an audit trail.

6 · Who it is for

One web portal. Three kinds of people.

🏦

Banks and wallet providers

Capture partner offers and link them to your cards and wallets, per card type or per wallet. Customers holding them get the alert.

🍽️

Restaurants, shops and spas

Add your premises on a map and publish offers for the general public. Set schedules, pause or end them any time.

📱

Everyday customers

Choose your cards, browse the map, favourite offers and get alerts as you walk by. No more leaflets.

Everything is managed from the web app:
  • Business profile and approval
  • Premises on a map
  • Discounts with a step-by-step wizard
  • Stats and Excel exports
