# POC privacy notice (draft, not legal advice)

Moz Discounts is a demonstration. By using it you agree that:
- We store your name, email, hashed password and the cards/wallets you choose (product only: never a card number, expiry, CVV or last four digits).
- Proximity alerts are decided on your phone. The server only receives alert events (type, premise, discount, time) with your position rounded to about 100 m.
- Businesses only see aggregated counts. Administrators can see alert events and the audit trail.
- You can delete your account in the app or portal: personal data is anonymised.
- Demo data (marked "Dados de demonstração / Demo data") is fictional.
A full legal review under Mozambican law is required before any real launch (spec assumption 12).
